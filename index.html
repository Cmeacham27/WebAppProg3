How to Make a PBJ

Instructions: Grab two pieces of bread, peanut butter, jelly, and a knife. Using the knife, scoop peanut butter and spread along both pieces of bread. Once the peanut is spread, scoop out jelly and spread it on one slice of bread. then put the two pieces of bread together and enjoy.
